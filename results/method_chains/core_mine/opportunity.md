# Cache opportunity scan

The frozen cache contains 90 target units, each with 500 eligible two-vehicle scenarios across five functions. It contains 9094 critical events, including 6291 collisions, and 5 target failures at candidates safe for every source.

Thus hit counts can saturate while archive coverage still has room: repeated failures in one function/region are discounted by CVS and F. The historically-safe target failures establish that target-local discrepancies exist, but are reported as a descriptive subgroup rather than a selection input.
