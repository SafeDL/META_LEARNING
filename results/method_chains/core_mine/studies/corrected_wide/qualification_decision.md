# v6 qualification decision

The independent qualification seed 20281209 failed the prespecified
dispersion gate. In 320 scenarios per target, corrected physical event counts
were 21 (idm_mobil), 41 (mcts_cv), 13 (ppo_ece), and 74 (vi_ttc), with ego
collision counts 11, 23, 1, and 25. Source-discordant events spanned multiple
modes and no controller triplet was redundant. However 90.5% of IDM events and
92.3% of PPO events fell in the shortest-gap quartile, above the frozen 75%
maximum. The full gate result is in `qualification/qualification_gate.json`.

The v6 development and validation seeds are therefore **not generated** and
no method-effect claim is made from v6. The qualification bank may be used as
development information for a separately versioned proposal with new seeds.
